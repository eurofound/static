About the data

Reading the figures

Job creation coverage. Announced job gains are only collected systematically from 2005 onwards, so gain and net-change figures before 2005 understate actual job creation.

United Kingdom. UK cases are no longer collected following the UK's withdrawal from the EU. UK figures reflect the period up to the end of 2020 only, and totals for later years exclude the UK entirely.

Small samples. Where a filter combination returns fewer than 30 cases the dashboard flags the result. Small counts largely reflect reporting coverage rather than actual restructuring activity, and shares computed on them are unstable.

Announced, not realised. Figures are announced planned changes at the time of the announcement; actual outcomes can differ, and large announcements are sometimes revised in later updates.

Coverage differences. The ERM is based on media reporting by national correspondents. Reporting intensity differs between countries and over time, so cross-country level comparisons should be made with care.
