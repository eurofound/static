Introduction of electric buses in Zagreb

Uvođenje elektricnih aubusa u Zagreb

Country Croatia , applies locally

  • Show NUTS regions


    • – HRZ EXTRA-REGIO NUTS 1
Time period Open ended, started on 21 October 2025
Context Green Transition
Type Other initiatives or policies
Category Promoting the economic, labour market and social recovery into a green future
– Sustainable mobility
Author Predrag Bejakovic (Faculty of Economics, Business, and Tourism (FEBT), University of Split) and Eurofound
Measure added 25 October 2025 (updated 10 December 2025)

Background information

On 21 October 2025, the remaining two of the total four contracted electric buses in Zagreb electric tram’s (Zagreb elektricni tramvaj – ZET) fleet were presented to mass-media and the general public. These are low-floor electric city buses, MAN LION’S CITY 12E, 12 meters in length, with a capacity for 71 passengers (35 seated and 36 standing). The total procurement value amounts to €1,242,000 (excluding VAT). This delivery marks the completion of ZET’s pilot project for fleet electrification and the company is now moving on to the procurement of another 70 electric buses, which will arrive by summer 2025. The public procurement process has been completed, there were no appeals, and there are no obstacles to implementing this project, within which ZET will acquire standard, articulated, and midi electric buses. The total estimated investment value is €50 million, with 90% of the funds expected to be provided through the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP).

Content of measure

The new buses are powered by an electric motor with 160 kW of power, while lithium-ion batteries with a total capacity of 480 kWh allow for a range of 330 km on a single charge. They feature a modern design and are equipped with air conditioning, ramps for easier access for persons with disabilities, USB ports for charging mobile devices, and four cameras for video surveillance of the passenger area. This procurement represents a step toward ZET’s historic transition to decarbonized bus transport, as the process of acquiring new electric buses through EU funds is in its final phase. In addition to the acquisition of electric buses, ZET signed a grant agreement at the end of September 2025 with the Ministry of Economy and the Environmental Protection and Energy Efficiency Fund for a project to build charging infrastructure for electric buses, worth €27,436,209, with completion scheduled for June 2016.

Updates

The following updates to this measure have been made after it came into effect.

11 November 2025

After 15 years, the Zagreb Electric Tram has received a completely new tram, manufactured by the Croatian company Končar-Electric Vehicles. The new low-floor tram NT2400 was put into trial operation after testing. It is the 14th of a total of 40 new NT2400 trams to be delivered under the current contract. The 20.8-meter-long NT2400 tram is equipped with batteries that charge during braking, which reduces electricity consumption and allows the tram to continue running in the event of a power failure, increasing service reliability. This model is designed with a low-floor entrance, making it easier for passengers, thereby including people with disabilities and parents with strollers, to enter and exit. A special platform for wheelchair entry is located at the second door area, ensuring additional accessibility. The interior of the tram is equipped with USB ports for charging mobile devices during the ride, and a stop announcement system and with new displays provide passengers with timely information about the route and stops. With a capacity of 115 passengers, including 27 seats, the NT2400 enables efficient transportation of a larger number of passengers. The maximum speed of 70 km/h allows faster and more efficient travel through the city. These features make the NT2400 a significant improvement in Zagreb's public transport, providing passengers with a safer, more comfortable and more environmentally friendly ride. A total of €200 million will be invested in the modernization of the tram network. The entire procurement of low-floor trams is fully financed through funds from the National Recovery and Resilience Plan.

Use of measure

The electrification of ZET’s fleet is beneficial for several reasons, the most important being the preservation of air quality in the city. “Traffic is one of the biggest sources of air pollution in cities. Shifting public transport vehicles from diesel to electric power will make a key contribution to improving air quality in Zagreb. Through the modernization of its fleet and the development of transport infrastructure, the City of Zagreb and ZET are actively following European and global environmental trends with the goal of achieving climate neutrality. This has also been recognized at the European level, so in November 2025 the European Commission awarded Zagreb the EU Mission Label for Climate-Neutral and Smart Cities. The city administration’s goal is to fully electrify ZET’s fleet within the next 10 years, meaning ZET will operate 500 electric buses.
