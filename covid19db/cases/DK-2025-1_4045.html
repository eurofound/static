Factsheet for measure DK-2025-1/4045 – measures in Denmark

Municipalities and businesses cooperate on implementing climate action plans.

Kommunerne og erhvervslivet går sammen om klimaplaner

Country Denmark , applies nationwide
Time period Temporary, 01 January 2025 – 31 December 2025
Context Green Transition, Extreme Weather Events
Type Other initiatives or policies
Category Promoting the economic, labour market and social recovery into a green future
– Strategic plans and programmes
Author Carsten Jørgensen (FAOS, University of Copenhagen), Louise Fabricius (Oxford Research) and Eurofound
Measure added 01 December 2025 (updated 23 December 2025)

Background information

All of the 98 municipalities in Denmark have committed to preparing climate action plans. As of 2025, 96 of the municipalities have completed their plans, and the remaining two are in progress. To strengthen implementation, a new partnership has been established between the Municipal Climate Action Committee and the business sector represented by Danish Industry (DI). The aim is to inspire and support local climate action, ensuring that municipal plans are translated into specific initiatives. The partnership focuses on three areas: sustainable public procurement to drive demand and support circular economy, backing for renewable energy projects, and climate reporting as a tool for reducing CO₂ emissions. The aim is to integrate business driven green solutions into municipal practices and promote dialogue where it is most needed.

Content of measure

The measure covers municipalities and local businesses working together on climate action. Eligibility applies to all Danish municipalities (98 in total) that have committed to preparing climate action plans, and to companies engaged in green solutions relevant for public procurement, renewable energy, or climate reporting. The partnership is primarily an organisational and collaborative initiative. No direct monetary support per case is specified. Funding or financial support is not detailed in total or per case; the measure focuses on knowledge sharing, dialogue, and integration of business solutions into municipal climate work. It aims to inspire local action and foster public-private cooperation on climate goals.

Use of measure

The measure does not specify direct payments to workers, citizens, or companies. All 98 municipalities are eligible participants, together with businesses engaged in green solutions. Uptake is currently expressed through the number of municipalities with completed climate action plans (96, with 2 underway). Support is provided through inspiration, best practice sharing, and a joint event highlighting the potential of green cooperation.

Involvement

The partnership was initiated jointly by the employers’ organisation (Confederation of Danish Industry) and the Municipal Climate Action Committee. Trade unions or employee representatives were not involved. The collaboration was not established through formal tripartite structures but through an institutionalised partnership between KL and the employers’ side. Social partner involvement applies primarily to the design phase; implementation and follow-up are carried out locally by municipalities, and no separate monitoring mechanisms involving social partners have been set up.

Views and reactions

The measure was initiated jointly by the employers’ organisation and KL, and has been positively received by the involved parties. The Confederation of Danish Industry supports the initiative and actively contributes to its implementation. No reactions from trade unions have been recorded, as they were not involved in the design or rollout. The measure is seen as a constructive step to strengthen local climate action through collaboration between municipalities and businesses.
