Factsheet for measure GR-2026-14/4287 – measures in Greece

Digital Fuel Card (Fuel Pass III)

Ψηφιακή κάρτα καυσίμων (FUEL PASS III)

Country Greece , applies nationwide
Time period Temporary, 01 April 2026 – 31 July 2026
Context Cost of Living Crisis
Type Other initiatives or policies
Category Measures to prevent social hardship
– Provision of services in kind (e.g. food vouchers)
Author Penny Georgiadou (INE GSEE)
Measure added 12 June 2026 (updated 26 June 2026)

Background information

Fuel Pass III was introduced as a temporary support measure for individuals and self-employed professionals, aimed at covering part of the increased cost of transport fuel and mobility during April and May 2026. The Prime Minister presented it as one of four measures designed to address rising energy costs, linked to the effects of the war in the Middle East and broader pressures on energy prices. The scheme was based on the Legislative Act of 25 March 2026, Government Gazette A’ 46, as ratified by Law 5292/2026 (Government Gazette A’ 53, 2 April 2026), and was further specified by Joint Ministerial Decision 52795/2026, Government Gazette B’ 1788, 31 March 2026.

Content of measure

The Fuel Pass III measure covers individuals, including self-employed professionals, who are tax residents of Greece and meet specific income criteria: up to € 25,000 for single persons; up to € 35,000 for married persons or parties to a civil partnership, increased by € 5,000 for each dependent child; and up to € 39,000 for single-parent families. Each individual could register only one car or motorcycle/moped, provided that the vehicle was in circulation, insured, and had no outstanding road tax liabilities. The support could be paid either through a digital debit card or into a bank account. For cars, the amounts were € 60 in island/special areas and € 50 in the rest of Greece when paid through a digital card, or € 50 and € 40 respectively when paid by bank transfer. For motorcycles and mopeds, the amounts were € 35 and € 30 through a digital card, or € 30 and € 25 by bank transfer. The total budget of the scheme was €130 million. A distinguishing feature of Fuel Pass III was that, in addition to fuel purchases, it could also be used for travel by public transport or taxi, increasing flexibility for beneficiaries. The card may be used until 31 July 2026, with no option for cash withdrawal.

Use of measure

The platform opened on 6 April 2026. Applications were submitted through vouchers.gov.gr or through Citizens’ Service Centres (KEP), with payments for the first applications made within 48 hours. The application period closed on 30 April 2026, while any remaining balance on the digital card remains valid until the end of July 2026. According to the published implementation report, 2,559,592 beneficiaries were included in Fuel Pass III, with total payments amounting to € 113,085,905. Of these, 1,597,127 chose payment into a bank account, while 962,465 used a digital debit card. No full assessment of the measure’s effectiveness has been published yet.
