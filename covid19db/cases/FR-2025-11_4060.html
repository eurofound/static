Factsheet for measure FR-2025-11/4060 – measures in France

The national climate change adaptation plan

Plan national d'adaptation au changement climatique

Country France , applies nationwide
Time period Open ended, started on 10 March 2025
Context Green Transition, Extreme Weather Events
Type Non-binding recommendations or other texts
Category Measures to prevent social hardship
– Other
Author Ambre Grenier-Boley (IR Share)
Measure added 02 December 2025 (updated 13 January 2026)

Background information

In 2006, France had established its first national adaptation strategy (Stratégie Nationale d'Adaptation). In 2011, the first national adaptation plan against climate change was introduced to progressively adapt to the global increase in temperatures. The plan covered a period of five years (until 2015), and included twenty fields of action, 242 measures, and 80 actions. Following the Paris Agreement, the plan was updated in 2018 through the "PNACC-2". In 2025, the plan was updated a third time.

Content of measure

The national plan is constituted of: * five key axes of action: 1. protecting individuals; 2. ensuring the resiliency of territories, infrastructure; and essential services; 3. adapting human activities; 4. protecting the cultural and natural heritage; 5. mobilising the nations' driving forces. * 52 measures, * two hundred steps.

The plan also includes the reference trajectory from 2024 to 2100 for global warming, based on the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) trend scenario, used as the common reference for metropolitan France.

The national plan does not refer to new funding sources. Indeed, many measures refer to the impact of previous implementations. The plan is related, however, to the pre-existing green funding source of €260 million, the €1 billion provided by water agencies, and the €300 million of the Barnier funds used for FR-2023-48/4035 MIRAPI Funds - ‘‘Rebuilding Better After Flooding" Case .

Use of measure

The first plan benefited from two follow-up plans. The first, of 14 June 2013, shows 96% of the 84 planned actions were begun. Similarly, 81% of the 240 measures were started. Lastly, the plan was evaluated in November 2015 by the General Council for the Environment and Sustainable Development (CGEDD). The report found that 80% of actions were implemented, and 75% of measures would be implemented at the end of 2016.

The second PNACC, based on the recommendations of the first plan, was also analysed halfway. It showed almost all actions had started being implemented, 53 out of 58 actions. Declined in 398 sub-measures, the 106 measures were implemented, 225 were in progress, and 58 had not been started. However, only 66 out of the 100 indicators used to follow the adaptation plan could be monitored.

The third PNACC, has been evaluated by the High Council for Climate, which has reported the need to strengthen the legal basis of governance systems for climate adaptation, the lack of integration of social policy, defence, and environmental, stronger scientific collaboration, as well as European and international cooperation.

Views and reactions

In 2024, the CFDT applauded the integration of a common referencing trajectory, and altered to the need for an implementation programme and financial means. For the CGT, in 2025 the plan remains extremely vague on various measures needed to tackle climate changes such as the lack of funds or inclusion of social partners in climate adaptation measures. The CFE-CGC, on the other hand, proposed in 2025 eleven points to ensure social dialogue enables environmental changes. The CFTC also called for a better social dialogue to ensure the green transition.
