Factsheet for measure FR-2026-22/4212 – measures in France

Extensions of payment deadlines of social security contributions

Délai de paiement des cotisations et contributions sociales

Country France , applies nationwide
Time period Open ended, started on 27 May 2026
Context Cost of Living Crisis
Type Other initiatives or policies
Category Supporting businesses to stay afloat
– Deferral of payments or liabilities
Author Ambre Grenier-Boley (IR Share)
Measure added 27 May 2026 (updated 26 June 2026)

Background information

In response to the important international tensions and the subsequent increase in the prices of fossil fuels, products, and agricultural inputs, the French government has implemented a support plan for several affected sectors.

One of the measures in this plan is an extension to the deadline for the payment of social security contributions. This is permitted by the Social Security Code. Employers and self-employed individuals, as well as employers of sailors and self-employed sailors, can defer the payment of contributions by up to 12 months. The deferral period can be increased under special circumstances. The self-employed can also reduce their provisional contributions if they anticipate lower business activity than the year prior.

Content of measure

The overall plan is expected to cost nearly €70 million by April 2026. However, there is no estimate for the cost of the extending payment deadlines for social security contributions.

Employers can apply for an extension of up to 12 months to payment deadlines, or longer in exceptional circumstances. To be eligible, employers must have paid their employees' contributions. Employers of sea workers, can also apply for an extension.

Additionally, self-employed workers can apply for an extension of up to 12 months, or longer in exceptional circumstances, to pay for their contributions. They can also request a reduction in their provisional contribution amounts if they expect their income to be lower than in the previous year's. Their contributions will then be adjusted at a later date. These workers can also apply to the social welfare scheme for the Social Protection Council's for the self-employed. Similarly, self-employed seafarers workers can also benefit from this extension.

A request for a deferral must be justified and include the following: * the background, including the amount of contributions, and the presence of any existing debt; * the cause and nature of the difficulty encountered; * the short-term measures implemented to improve the financial position of the company or self-employed individual; * an estimated timeline of the period that is necessary for recovery, and the duration of the deferral; * any other useful documents should also be included.

Use of measure

No information of the estimated number of workers or companies eligible to extend payment deadlines is available.

Involvement

The social partners were not involved in the design, implementation, or monitoring of the measure.

Views and reactions

The social partners did not specifically react to the measure regarding the extension of social security contribution payment deadlines. However, several trade union confederations and federations, as well as a few employer organisations, have criticised the government's overall support plan.

For instance, one of the CFDT's federations called for additional support for public servants. The CGT confederation, on the other hand, called on the government to convene an emergency roundtable to discuss rising energy prices. The CGT also called for strict price controls, a floating energy tax and a levy on the profits of energy companies and very high-income households. Similarly, the National Road Transport Federation criticised the plans, stating that they were late and inadequate compared to the responses of other European Member States. However, the employer organisation CPME supported the government's strategy of supporting the economy without significantly increasing public spending.
