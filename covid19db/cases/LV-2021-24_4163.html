Factsheet for measure LV-2021-24/4163 – measures in Latvia

Prevention of Flood and Coastal Erosion Risk Threats in Urban Areas

Plūdu un krasta erozijas risku apdraudējumu novēršana pilsētu teritorijās

Country Latvia , applies nationwide
Time period Temporary, 10 June 2021 – 01 January 2026
Context Green Transition, Extreme Weather Events
Type Other initiatives or policies
Category Promoting the economic, labour market and social recovery into a green future
– Other
Author Santa Barone-Upeniece (Civitta) and Eurofound
Measure added 19 December 2025 (updated 14 January 2026)

Background information

Flooding and coastal erosion increasingly threaten urban infrastructure, environmental quality and residents’ well-being in Latvia, with climate change amplifying these risks. To reduce damage to settlements, critical infrastructure and the Baltic Sea coastal environment, Latvia introduced targeted investment measures during the 2014–2020 EU programming period to strengthen flood protection, mitigate shoreline erosion and improve climate resilience in cities. It was implemented under the EU Cohesion Policy 2014–2020, within the Operational Programme 'Growth and Employment', Specific Objective 5.1.1: 'Prevent flood and coastal erosion risk threats in urban areas.'

Content of measure

The measure supports reconstruction and construction of hydraulic structures, nature-based solutions and green infrastructure to reduce flood and coastal erosion risks in urban territories. Investments targeted both sea-coast erosion reduction to protect critical infrastructure and flood-protection measures in flood-prone settlements. The aim was to reduce the number of residents and contaminated or potentially contaminated sites exposed to flood and erosion hazards. Supported activities included:

Restoration of municipal stormwater and drainage systems; Reconstruction and construction of dikes and protective embankments; River and lake cleaning to improve flow and reduce inundation; Wetland restoration and management to enhance natural water retention; Green infrastructure solutions such as rain gardens and permeable drainage features; Coastal protection works and erosion-control measures.

The total investment in the measure is €39 million. The measure was co-financed by the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF) with a total investment of €24 million.

Use of measure

Overall, the programme benefits more than 180,000 residents living in nationally significant flood-risk territories.

Preliminary Flood Risk Assessment 2025 lists following projects done under the measure:

  1. Flood risk reduction measures in the city of Ventspils Total funding: €1,753,845.35 EU funding: €1,476,305.26 Completion year: 2020

  2. Reconstruction of the Jelgava airfield polder embankment to prevent flood threats Total funding: €3,905,655.13 EU funding: €1,814,750.00 Completion year: 2020

  3. Implementation of complex measures to restore the flow capacity of the Svēte River and reduce flood hazards in adjacent areas (Jelgava) Total funding: €2,933,426.23 EU funding: €1,785,161.30 Completion year: 2020

  4. Prevention of flood and coastal erosion risks in the territory of Ogre city by reconstructing the non-flooded protective embankment of the Ogre River Total funding: €1,399,236.93 EU funding: €785,970.33 Completion year: 2015

  5. Prevention of flood and coastal erosion risks in Ogre city by rebuilding the old embankment and constructing a new breakwater (flow deflector) at the confluence of the Ogre River with the Daugava Total funding: €5,336,602.53 EU funding: €3,569,698.36 Completion year: 2022

  6. Construction of the Daugavpils city protective embankment on the right bank of the Daugava River, in the Nometņu Street area, a flood-prone territory Total funding: €3,443,839.61 EU funding: €2,202,697.04 Completion year: 2022

  7. Flood-protection measures in Bolderāja Total funding: €4,608,200.85 EU funding: €3,662,438.05 Completion year: 2022

  8. Construction of groynes in the Baltic Sea (Liepāja) Total funding: €7,638,281.85 EU funding: €4,621,258.33 Planned completion: 2025 (main works completed in 2023)

  9. Measures to eliminate flood and coastal erosion risks caused by the Lielupe River in Dubulti–Majori–Dzintari Total funding: €5,215,020.55 EU funding: €2,072,442.70 Planned completion: 2026

  10. Prevention of flood and coastal erosion risks in Ādaži municipality, first stage Total funding: €3,551,843.95 EU funding: €896,265.00 Completion year: 2024
