Factsheet for measure CY-2025-31/4092 – measures in Cyprus

Package of supporting and reconstructing measures for areas affected by the fire and fire victims

Background information

On 30th July 2025, the Council of Ministers announced a package of measures for fire victims and the areas affected by the fire that erupted on 23 July 2025 and lasted for two days in the mountainous areas of the district of Limassol. The fire cost the live of two people, destroyed 700 buildings, burned a large number of animals and more than 100 square kilometres causing enormous damage of the natural resources of the region. According to the Cyprus Scientific and Technical Chamber (ΕΤΕΚ), the total estimated cost for restoring the buildings is around €40 million, of which approximately 90% is related with the cost of restoring buildings and structures that were completely destroyed by the fire. The package of supporting measures is supported by the EU Solidarity Fund. On 6 December 2025, an advance payment of €2.3 million was announced by the Commission.

Content of measure

The different measures of the package, the eligible beneficiaries and the support go as follows:

A. A lump sum payment for the basic needs of families whose primary residence has been partially or totally destroyed: - Totally destroyed: €10,000 plus 2,000 for each depended child - Partially destroyed: €5,000 plus 1,000 for each depended child - Owners of vacation residences are not eligible for the lump sum B. Rent Subsidy: €800 plus €200 for each depended child per quarter: for totally destroyed residences, the maximum period of the subsidy is 24 months, for partially destroyed and uninhabitable residences 6 months and for renders of damage residences 3 months. C. Full compensation for damaged properties: o The payment of support for the totally destroyed licenced residences come in three instalments: 25% upon application, 25% upon presentation of a statement by a surveyor and 50% by the completion of the 50% of the work. - For partially damaged residences: the repair cost is paid in a lump sum before the work begins after the restoration cost assessment by ETEK. D. Fee exemptions: permanent residences and businesses operating in the affected areas are exempt from community fees and taxes for 2025. The cost will be paid to Community Councils by the state.
E. Suspension of rent payments: it concerns the properties that have been granted by the state for agricultural or livestock use in the affected areas. F. Financial support for Community Councils: it covers emergency needs of the community G. Re-issuing of documents (e.g., IDs, passports, birth certificates) destroyed by the fire free from fees. H. Restoration of water supply and flood protection works: €1.55 million
I. Compensation and reactivation plan for affected farmers and livestock breeders: €23.5 million J. Direct support for affected businesses up to €300,000 per business for: - Compensation for goods, raw materials and supplies destroyed by the fire - Financial assistance for the restoration of affected infrastructure and equipment K. Financial support for workers affected by the fire: it concerns employees and self-employed persons whose work has been interrupted. It provides full salary based on the last salary paid, as declared in the Social Insurance Fund.
L. Tourist sector: one-off paid of €1,000 per bed for tourist accommodation operating in traditional buildings and an additional financial aid of 40% based on the turnover for the second half of 2024.

Use of measure

According to the latest update of the Presidency on 28 August 2025, the development of the reconstructing measures goes as follows:

  • Restoration of residences: a. the list of equipment costs per residence has been completed, b. completion of cost estimates by ETEK for approximately 331 partially damage residences, and c. 160 applications have been assessed and promoted for payment of first instalment
  • Rent subsidy: 115 applications have been submitted
  • Lump sum payments: €2.2 million paid for 279 residences
  • Restoration of damaged vehicles and machinery: the recording of 219 damage vehicles and 353 machines have been completed
  • Exemptions from community fees and taxes: start of data collection from Community Councils
  • Financial assistance was provided for 189 to cover emergency, basic or special needs
  • Financial support for workers: the payment of €232.825 to cover the salaries of 194 workers for the period 23 July to 31 August 2025 has been completed
  • Business support: an amount of €39,760 has been paid out, relating to advance payments in 14 cases, while the recording and costing of the losses of the affected businesses continues.
  • Farmers and livestock breeders: the payment of €3 million in compensation to 1,266 individuals has been completed by 85%
  • Restoration of infrastructures: 966 applications have been received
  • Tourist sector: the payment of subsidies (06/08/2025) to a total of 27 eligible companies was completed, with a total expenditure of €136,000
  • Community Councils: the amounts were paid on 25/7/2025, while additional needs will be covered in consultation with the Community Councils
  • Water supply and electricity: fully restored
  • The Environment Department has already secured the cooperation of licensed units for the collection and management of waste and is in full communication with the local authorities to assist in the removal and disposal of waste.
  • Flood protection: the Department of Agriculture identified approximately 60 locations in the waterways of the fired area where flood protection works are required, which will begin in early September, with the most dangerous locations to be completed by the end of October
  • Wildlife management and restoration: planting during the autumn period in collaboration with Community Councils and organised groups (around 10-20 thousand plants) with an estimated cost of €25-50,000 and a maintenance cost for 3 years ranging from €425-850,000. ) with an estimated cost of €25-50,000 and a maintenance cost for 3 years ranging from €425-850,000.

Involvement

The package of measures was designed by the members of the Ministerial Council without the involvement of social partners. The design preceded the documentation of the damages by the competent Ministries and state agencies, the cost assessment of buildings by the Cyprus Scientific and Technical Chamber (ETEK) and the meeting of the President of the Republic with leaders of the local community councils affected by the fire. Each of the measure of the package is implement by the competent Ministry. For example, the Ministry of Interior implements the measures related with lump sum and rent subsidy, the compensation of damaged properties, the re-issuing of documents, the fee exemptions and the financial support of community councils. The Ministry of Agriculture implements the measure for the restoration of water supply and flood protection works and the Deputy Ministry of Tourism, the measure related with the tourist accommodation. The Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry implements the measure for the support of businesses and the Ministry of Labour and Social Insurances implements the measures related with the financial support of workers.

Views and reactions

Social partners and political parties appeared satisfied with the announcement of the measures, the extent, approach and objectives of each but very critical with the President of the Republic. They accused the President because he has never taken the responsibility on behalf of the government for the delays in fighting the fire, the lack of coordination between the state services and the extent of the damage of the fire despite the President’s claims for the readiness of the state to deal with the fires shortly before.
